# Overview
This interactive Sales Dashboard provides comprehensive analytics for e-commerce and retail operations, enabling business stakeholders to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, and operational metrics in real-time.

# Key Features
## 📊 Sales Metrics
Total Quantity Sold: Track overall sales volume

Total Price by Quantity: Revenue analysis based on product quantity

Order Status Distribution: Monitor fulfillment status (Delivered, Shipped, Pending, Cancelled, Returned)

## 💳 Payment Analytics
Payment Method by Referral Source: Analyze how different marketing channels influence payment preferences

Payment Method by Referral Status: Understand payment behavior across customer segments

## 📦 Product Performance
Items in Cart by Product: Track popular products and shopping cart activity

Top Products: Monitor, Laptop, Phone, Tablet, Desk, Chair, Printer

## 🎯 Customer Insights
Referral Source Analysis: Performance metrics for Instagram, Email, Google, Facebook, and Referral channels

Customer Journey Tracking: From referral to purchase completion
